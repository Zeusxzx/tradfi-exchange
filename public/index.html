What this is

A coin that keeps
market hours.

You can only trade $TRADFI while the New York Stock Exchange is open. After the closing bell the contract itself refuses the trade.

Robinhood Chain · Uniswap v4

Three things, then you can go.

  1. It is a memecoin. Plain ERC-20. No tax, no owner, no transfer hooks.
  2. It only trades 9:30–4, weekdays. New York hours. Closed on NYSE holidays, 1:00 on half days.
  3. The contract enforces it, not us. Outside those hours a swap reverts on-chain. Nobody can turn that off.
