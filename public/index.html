What this is

A coin that keeps
market hours.

You can only trade $TRADFI while the New York Stock Exchange is open. After the closing bell the contract itself refuses the trade.

Robinhood Chain · Uniswap v4

The market closes. So does the coin.

9:30 AM to 4:00 PM, New York time. Outside those hours, nobody trades it.

    It keeps hours

    Opens at 9:30 AM. Shuts at 4:00 PM. New York time.

  2. 02

    Nobody holds a key

    The hours live in the contract. At 9:30 it opens itself, at 4:00 it closes itself.

  3. 03

    Miss it and you wait

    Try to buy at 4:01 and the chain turns you down. Come back when the bell rings.

    The swap reverts with MarketClosed(nextOpen)
